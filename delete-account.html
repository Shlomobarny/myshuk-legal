MyShuk - Delete your account

You can delete your MyShuk account at any time, in either of two ways:

1. In the app (fastest) Open MyShuk and sign in, then go to Settings, tap Delete account, type DELETE to confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

2. By email Write to myshukapp@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We delete it and confirm within 7 days.

What is deleted Your profile, your listings, homes, jobs, businesses and statuses, your saved items, the photos and videos you uploaded, and your conversations. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What may remain for a short time Copies in system backups, removed automatically on a short cycle, and anything the law requires us to keep. Messages you already sent to another person can remain in that person’s inbox.

You do not need to sign in to this page to delete your account.
